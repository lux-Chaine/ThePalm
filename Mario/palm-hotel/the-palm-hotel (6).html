The Palm Hotel · Kafr El Sheikh

A refined four-star address in the heart of the Nile Delta, The Palm pairs contemporary comfort with the warmth of Egyptian hospitality. Forty-eight rooms and suites, three dining destinations, and 250 m² of event space sit minutes from the University, the Museum, and the city's commercial district — making The Palm the address of choice for business travel, family visits, and celebrations alike.

Good to Know

Policies at a glance.

Check-in / Check-out

Check-in from 14:00, check-out until 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are offered subject to availability.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival for standard-rate bookings; non-refundable rates are noted at booking.

Children & Extra Beds

Children of all ages are welcome. Extra beds and cots are available on request, subject to room capacity and a surcharge.

Payment & ID

Visa and Mastercard accepted alongside cash. A valid national ID or passport is required for check-in.

Pets

For the comfort of all guests, pets are not permitted within the hotel premises.

Smoking

The Palm is a non-smoking property; designated outdoor smoking areas are available.
